# AI Tool Usage

This assessment made limited use of AI tools as development support. ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Claude (Anthropic) were used mainly when I became stuck, needed help understanding bugs or errors, or needed guidance with specific development tasks. The application was primarily designed and developed by me.

**Debugging and troubleshooting.** The main use of AI assistance was to help identify and understand bugs and errors encountered during development. When I became stuck with an Android Studio, coding, build, or configuration issue, AI was used to explain the possible cause of the problem and suggest solutions. I then applied and tested the solutions myself rather than relying on AI to develop the application for me.

**Creating the application icon.** ChatGPT was used to help create the application's icon. I provided instructions describing how I wanted the icon to look, including the YouthLink name, the design concept, symbols, and the colours that I wanted to use. The generated design was then used as the basis for the application's icon.

**Choosing a deployment application.** Claude was used for guidance when deciding which application or service could be used to deploy the backend. It helped me compare the available options and understand which option would be suitable for my project, including the use of Render.

**Getting unstuck during development.** AI assistance was occasionally used when I was unsure how to proceed with a particular problem or implementation issue. The AI provided explanations and possible approaches that helped me understand the problem and continue working on the application.

**What was not AI-generated.** The original application idea, research, design decisions, feature requirements, user interface direction, and overall development of the application were completed by me. AI was not used to create the complete application or replace my own development work.

All AI suggestions were reviewed and tested by me before being used. I remained responsible for the final code, design, testing, functionality, and decisions made throughout the development of the application.
